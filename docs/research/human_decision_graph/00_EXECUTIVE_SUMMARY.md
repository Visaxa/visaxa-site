# Executive Summary

## Answer

If Visaxa wants to become a knowledge source used during AI-assisted research, it should become authoritative about the **human decisions that precede product comparison**, with atomic concepts and evidence methods underneath those decisions.

The recommended model is:

`human decision → required concepts → decision-changing evidence → article/method → next decision → product comparison`

Keywords remain useful for discovery and measurement, but should not define the knowledge architecture. Concepts remain essential, but should not be the only user-facing navigation. Product comparison belongs at the end.

## What the new graph adds

The previous research began with questions already being asked. This study reconstructs the earlier path and identifies 30 human decision nodes from first problem recognition through implementation, routine use, recovery and safe exit.

The master path begins with:

1. Is the problem recurring and systemic?
2. Is its cost or risk high enough to act?
3. Can process or policy fix it without new software?
4. What operating outcome must change?
5. Is the business ready to standardize and implement?

It reaches product comparison only at D21, after decisions about system boundary, operational constraints, access, portability, evidence, future cost, adoption, report proof, failure handling and exit readiness.

## Different decision paths exist

Six owner paths are documented:

- solo professional;
- owner opening a first business;
- first salon owner with staff;
- growing salon;
- multi-location owner;
- owner replacing failed software.

They share nodes but enter in different places and assign different weight to cost, flexibility, staff impact, centralized control and recovery.

## Emotion findings

The coded corpus is dominated by fear (31 rows), frustration (25), overwhelm (16), cost anxiety (14), uncertainty (12) and distrust (9). These are sample signals, not market statistics.

The key emotional conclusion is that trust should be treated as a result of verification. For a failed-system replacement owner, the path is commonly:

`frustration → fear → regret/anger → urgency → risk aversion → relief or repeated regret`

Therefore recovery, reconciliation and reversibility evidence matter more than reassuring comparison language.

## Five connected graphs

1. **Human Decision Graph:** 30 decisions and six owner-profile paths.
2. **Emotion Graph:** observed drivers and interpreted desired state transitions.
3. **AI Reasoning Graph:** clarification, concepts, evidence and trusted source types for every decision.
4. **Concept Graph:** maps each decision to one or several reusable concept nodes.
5. **Article Graph:** 24 linked article roles, with product comparison at A24 rather than A09.

Information flows as:

`fear/state → decision → AI clarification → concept(s) → evidence → article → next decision`

## Current Visaxa position

The five published articles currently own parts of these decisions:

- CRM evaluation: D10/D15/D21;
- salon CRM choice: D10/D16/D17/D20/D21;
- scheduling failure: D01/D03/D12;
- scheduling scale: D12/D28;
- employee adoption: D24/D27.

The main failure is not that these subjects are wrong. Their decision paths terminate. Visaxa lacks the next layers for portability, evidence standards, report proof, migration acceptance, go-live acceptance, offboarding, recovery and safe decommissioning.

## Recommended next branches

1. **What must be true before product comparison?** D10–D14.
2. **Did implementation actually work?** D22–D26.
3. **What evidence earns commitment?** D15–D21.

These branches connect naturally to current evaluation and scheduling content while adding the missing proof layer.

## Known versus inferred

- **Known:** corpus stages, coded emotional drivers, current published articles, internal links and repeated decision problems.
- **Interpretation:** the normalized 30-node sequence and owner-profile routing.
- **Hypothesis:** decision-led flagships plus atomic concept pages will improve usefulness in AI-assisted research.
- **Requires testing:** citation behavior, assisted-search retrieval, owner navigation and whether first-time owners exhibit the proposed hope-to-overwhelm transition.

## Final strategic rule

Visaxa should not start by asking, “Which software topic should we publish next?”

It should ask:

“Which unresolved human decision prevents the owner from moving safely to the next decision, and what evidence would resolve it?”
